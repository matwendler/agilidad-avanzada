# IDEO.org — Design Kit (Human-Centered Design)

Plataforma de IDEO.org: herramientas, métodos y mindsets de diseño centrado en las personas. "Reached over 1 million creative problem solvers around the world."

Se organiza en: **Mindsets | Methods | Case Studies | Resources**.

Materiales principales:
- **Field Guide to Human-Centered Design** — "a step-by-step guide that will get you solving problems like a designer" (online y en papel).
- **Design Kit: Travel Pack** — mazo de 32 ejercicios de diseño.
- **Case studies** — p. ej. *Asili*, empresa comunitaria de salud, agricultura y agua en la RD del Congo.

## Métodos por fase

### Fase 1 — Inspiration (inspiración / descubrimiento)

Frame Your Design Challenge · Define Your Audience · Align on Your Impact Goals · Create a Project Plan · Build a Team · Recruiting Tools · Secondary Research · Expert Interview · Interview · Group Interview · Extremes and Mainstreams · Immersion · Guided Tour · Peers Observing Peers · Photojournal · Collage · Card Sort · Draw It · Resource Flow · Body Language · The Five Whys · Conversation Starters · Analogous Inspiration · Setting Up Co-Designers to Facilitate Interviews · Align on Your Participatory Approach · Power Mapping · Download Your Learnings · Share Inspiring Stories

Citas útiles:
- *Photojournal*: "Photos are a fantastic way to learn about a person's life, especially if they're the one taking them."
- *Immersion*: "understand the people you're designing for by immersing yourself".
- *Guided Tour*: "reveal their habits and values far better than talking".
- *The Five Whys*: "uncover the deep motivations and assumptions".

### Fase 2 — Ideation (ideación)

Create Insight Statements · Create Frameworks ("visual representation of a system") · Collaborative Synthesis · Find Themes · How Might We · Brainstorm · Brainstorm Rules (las 7 reglas) · Brainwriting implícito · Bundle Ideas · Get Visual · Mash-Ups · Top Five · Design Principles · Explore Your Hunch · Co-Creation Session · Determine What to Prototype · Get Feedback · Headlines from the Future · Group Chat Workshop

### Fase 3 — Implementation (implementación)

Create a Concept · Rapid Prototyping · Role Play · Storyboard · Build & Run Prototypes · Participatory Prototyping · Integrate Feedback and Iterate · Journey Map ("identify and strategize for key moments") · Gut Check · Live Prototyping · Pilot · Keep Iterating · Keep Getting Feedback · Business Model Canvas · Ways to Grow Framework · Capabilities Quicksheet · Staff Your Project · Create a Pitch · Roadmap for Success · Sustainable Revenue · Build Partnerships · Funding Strategy · Monitor and Evaluate · Define Your Indicators · Explore Your Theory of Change · Create a Logic Model · Explore Scalability · Optimize and Adapt for Scale

## Cómo mapea al proceso de la materia

| Etapa del programa | Fase Design Kit |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| Descubrimiento | Inspiration |
| Definición del problema | Inspiration → Ideation (Insight Statements, Find Themes, Frameworks) |
| Ideación de soluciones | Ideation (Brainstorm, How Might We, Mash-Ups) |
| Selección de alternativas | Ideation (Top Five, Bundle Ideas, Gut Check) |
| Prototipado | Implementation (Rapid Prototyping, Storyboard, Role Play) |
| Evaluación | Implementation (Get Feedback, Live Prototyping, Pilot, Monitor & Evaluate) |
